# Storyboards

All four videos are 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. Battle and level-up footage is shown as a framed screen card over a blurred copy of itself, so captions sit above it.
Captions sit inside the social safe zone: 230 px from the top,
440 px from the bottom and 140 px from the right edge are kept clear. Every gameplay shot is a real recording of the
game (`assets/recordings/`). The source of each shot is named under **Shot**.

**Labels on screen:**
- **REAL GAMEPLAY:** recorded live play.
- **REAL GAME WORLD / 50 THINGS TO BUILD:** the game's own 3D scenes and models, filmed in the development world lab with a cinematic camera.
- **COMING SOON:** built but not online yet (multiplayer).
- **PLANNED:** not built (clans, leaderboards).
