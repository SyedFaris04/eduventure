## Introductory post (attach Video A)
What if studying was an adventure?

We're building EDUVENTURE: a learning RPG where every monster asks a real question, right answers power your attack,
and you level up by learning. It's free and playable now in early access, on the web and Android.

It's not finished, and we'd love your help shaping it. 🧵

## Thread (reply under the post)
1/ The idea: most study apps feel like homework with points. We wanted the learning to be the gameplay, so the
question is the attack and a wrong answer explains why.

2/ What's in it today: 5 islands, 600+ questions in 25 subjects, a Code Academy (Python and JavaScript), a Language
Hall (English and Bahasa Melayu), a pet that grows when you learn, and your own island to build and decorate.

3/ What's coming: playing with friends is built and coming online soon. Clans and leaderboards are on the list, but
only if you want them.

4/ So, a few honest questions:
• Would you play an educational RPG?
• Which feature interests you most?
• Which subjects should we add?
• Solo or multiplayer missions?
• What would make you come back every day?
• What would you improve?

5/ Try it free → syedfaris04.github.io/eduventure
