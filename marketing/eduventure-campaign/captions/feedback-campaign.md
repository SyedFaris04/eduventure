# Early access and feedback campaign

## The invitation (website, bio, pinned posts)
EDUVENTURE is being built for students around the world, starting with Malaysia's KSSR and KSSM topics.

We want to make learning more exciting through RPG gameplay, exploration, challenges and community.
It's free and playable now in early access, and we're looking for early testers who want to help shape it.

Your ideas matter. Come explore with us. → syedfaris04.github.io/eduventure

## Recommended feedback form (no form exists yet; create one, then put its link in the videos' `feedbackForm` field)
Keep it short (about 2 minutes). **Collect no names, school names, emails or phone numbers from children.**
Contact details are optional, for adults only.

1. **Age range:** under 13 · 13–17 · 18–24 · 25+. If under 13, the form should end with "Thanks! Ask a parent to fill in the rest" and collect nothing else.
2. **Country** (list)
3. **Education level:** primary · lower secondary · upper secondary / SPM · pre-university · university · not studying
4. **Did you try the game?** yes · no, just watched
5. **Favourite feature** (pick one): battles with questions · My island · pet · Code Academy · Language Hall · islands · festivals
6. **Least interesting feature** (same list, plus "none")
7. **Subjects you want added** (free text, short)
8. **Preferred way to play:** solo · with friends · both
9. **What would make you come back every day?** (free text)
10. **Interested in testing new versions?** yes · no
11. **Anything else we should improve?** (free text)
12. **Optional, adults 18+ only: an email if you'd like updates.** Leave blank to stay anonymous.

Free tools that work: Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. Turn off "collect email addresses" and sign-in requirements.
