# Video A — Main announcement trailer (43 s) · voice-over script

The rendered videos have no narration: the captions carry the message, so they work with the sound off (most social
viewers watch muted). Record this script if you want a narrated version (see documentation/editing-guide.md, "Adding a
voice-over"). Tone: curious, friendly, energetic — a student telling a friend about a game, not an advert.
About 95 words: it fits at a natural pace, with pauses where the music breathes.

| Time | Picture | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | The portal | "What if studying… was actually an adventure?" |
| 4–9 s | Logo, tagline | "Meet EDUVENTURE." *(beat)* "Learn. Explore. Grow. Conquer." |
| 9–16 s | Battle, rewards, level up | "Every monster asks a real question. Answer it right — and your attack lands. Earn XP, collect gold, level up." |
| 16–24 s | Creator, pet, harvest, island | "Make your own hero. Hatch a pet that grows as you learn. Harvest, build, and decorate your own island." |
| 24–32 s | Islands, camp, friends, festivals | "Explore five islands, build camps together — and soon, play with your friends." |
| 32–36 s | Village orbit | "We're building something different… and we want you to help shape it." |
| 36–43 s | End card | "EDUVENTURE. Free in early access, on the web and Android. Try it — and tell us what to improve." |

**Honesty notes for the narrator:** say "soon" for playing with friends (multiplayer isn't online yet); don't mention
clans or leaderboards as features (they're planned); "early access" and "free" are accurate today.
