# Turn forgetting into EDUVENTURE's daily adventure

EDUVENTURE gets players back every day by making *remembering* the daily quest. A server-side scheduler brings back concepts the player has already beaten as "Returning monsters", delivered through the daily missions that already exist. Elo ratings tune battles to roughly 75–80% success. Streaks forgive a missed day, and the knowledge pet grows only through learning and player level and never suffers. The evidence supports this. The biggest retention wins in learning apps came from mechanics that **protect habits, add social structure and tie progress to mastery**. The two learning techniques rated "high utility", practice testing and spaced practice, are exactly what EDUVENTURE's question-powered combat already does. What changes is when and which questions come back. The ethical limits are now written into law and app store policy. Regulators in the EU, UK and US name streaks, guilt-tripping characters, countdown timers, obscured virtual currencies and paid random rewards as harmful. Brazil has banned loot boxes for minors since March 2026. Malaysia's Child Protection Code (in force 1 June 2026) sets the local benchmark: highest-privacy defaults, no contact between children and adult strangers, and parental controls. The owner's two pet requests can be met safely. Evolution requires learning points plus player level. Gold pays a token ceremony fee and buys fixed-price cosmetics. Gold stays earn-only, so nobody can buy their way around learning. Malaysia also gives EDUVENTURE an opening no global competitor has. Parents lost the UPSR and PT3 benchmarks, teachers use DELIMa but students mostly don't, Roblox was nearly banned, and no local curriculum-mapped 3D world exists. The report ends with 9 "build next", 9 "build soon" and 7 "later" features, plus a do-not-build list.

## Protecting habits and adding social structure drove Duolingo's 4.5x growth

The best-documented turnaround in learning apps came from optimising one number. When Duolingo's growth slowed, the team made **current-user retention (CURR)**, the share of active users who come back the next day, its North Star. In its growth model, CURR moved DAU **5x more than the next-best lever**. Over four years CURR rose 21%, daily churn among the best users fell by more than 40%, and **DAU grew 4.5x** ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)). By Q2 2026 CURR had reached an all-time high of **84%**, with 58.7M DAU ([Duolingo Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)). The failures matter as much. A Gardenscapes-style moves counter copied from puzzle games was **completely neutral**, and a referral offer raised new users by only 3% ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)). Mechanics borrowed from entertainment games don't transfer automatically.

The streak results consistently show that **forgiveness beats punishment**. Separating the daily goal from the streak raised D14 retention by 3.3% ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)). Learners offered a Weekend Amulet were 4% more likely to return a week later and 5% less likely to lose their streak. A Streak Wager paid in gems lifted **D7 retention by 14%** ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals)). A one-off amnesty in June 2026 revived **15.4M streaks**, nearly 8M of them from learners with no active streak at the time ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)). The strongest independent evidence comes from an RCT with about 60,000 Peruvian students in grades 4–6. Highlighting streaks on a maths platform increased use **and significantly improved maths achievement**, while personalised reminders worked better at getting non-users to start ([NBER w34173](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34173)). Duolingo's own staff add two caveats. The link between long streaks and low drop-off is "largely correlation and selection bias", and streaks "can't make up for a weak product" ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks)).

Leagues are the second big lever, and they show how engagement metrics go wrong. Weekly leaderboards with promotion and demotion raised **learning time by 17%** and roughly **tripled highly engaged learners** ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)). They also invited XP farming. In 2024 Duolingo re-weighted XP toward lessons along the learning path, which added **1.1–1.8M learning minutes a day**. It dropped "total sessions" as a metric because it favoured grinding on short, easy sessions, and found that shorter lessons ultimately reduced time spent learning well ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/time-spent-learning-well)). EDUVENTURE can skip that detour by defining its core metric around learning from day one.

The closest analogue to EDUVENTURE is a warning. Prodigy Math wraps maths in a fantasy RPG where each spell needs a correct answer. Its efficacy evidence is vendor-commissioned and correlational, rated ESSA Tier 3, for example 0.73 state-test points per extra hour ([Prodigy](https://www.prodigygame.com/blog/council-bluffs)). In 2021 about 20 advocacy groups asked the FTC to act after counting **16 membership ads and only 4 maths problems in a 19-minute session**, for a membership costing up to $107 a year ([Fairplay](https://fairplayforkids.org/feb-19-2021-advocates-to-ftc-prodigy-math-game-preys-on-kids-and-families/)). Duolingo's 2025 switch from Hearts to an Energy system that drains on every exercise and refills through ads or payment provoked petitions and quitting ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/)). Any mechanic that stops learning until someone pays is the line not to cross with children.

Classroom tools and meta-analyses point the same way. A review of 93 Kahoot studies found mostly positive effects. It also found that speed scoring causes "stressful time-pressure", guessing and reduced reflection ([Wang & Tahir 2020](https://research.gold.ac.uk/id/eprint/39435)), which hurts slower and second-language learners, a real concern in Malaysia's multilingual classrooms. Khan Academy users averaging **30+ minutes a week** grew about 20% more than expected (effect size 0.36, correlational) ([Khan Academy](https://2023-2024.annualreport.khanacademy.org/efficacy-results)). Students chased **repetitive badges** most ([Ruipérez-Valiente et al.](https://webs.um.es/jruiperez/papers/conferences/2016_LAK_Intentionality_postprint.pdf)). So the learning effect comes from **dosage plus mastery**, not decorations. Across meta-analyses, gamification gives a cognitive effect of about **g = 0.49**, and **game fiction and social interaction** are the moderators that matter ([Sailer & Homner 2020](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/frontdoor/index/index/docId/109056)). Points, badges and leaderboards on their own raise output quantity only ([Mekler et al. 2017](https://glassmanlab.seas.harvard.edu/annotated_works/mekler17.pdf)). A 16-week course that added only a leaderboard and badges **lowered intrinsic motivation and final exam scores** ([Hanus & Fox, via UCLA review](https://www.smhp.psych.ucla.edu/pdfdocs/gamil.pdf)).

Self-determination research explains why. Across 128 studies, expected tangible rewards undermined later free-choice motivation, and **more for children (d = −0.44) than for college students (d = −0.25)**. Informational positive feedback improved it (d = +0.33) ([Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999](https://depts.washington.edu/techdocs/papers/deciExtrinsicRewardsAndIntrinsicMotivation99.pdf)). Unexpected rewards were less harmful than expected ones. A children's game should therefore reward through competence feedback, choice and co-op, use surprise rewards and cosmetics, and never pay children simply for logging in.

## Spaced retrieval is an engagement engine EDUVENTURE already has

Of ten common study techniques, **practice testing and distributed practice were the only two rated "high utility"**. Rereading, highlighting and summarising were rated low ([APS on Dunlosky et al. 2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade.html)). EDUVENTURE's monster battles are practice tests. A student who studied once and was tested three times recalled **61% after a week, against 40%** for one who studied four times ([WashU](https://source.washu.edu/?p=55770)). Restudying wins only on a test five minutes later ([Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://psychology.ecu.edu/wp-content/pv-uploads/sites/216/2019/03/Roediger-Karpicke-2006.pdf)). A review of 50 classroom experiments from primary school to university found **57% of effects were medium or large** ([Agarwal et al., OSF](https://osf.io/mz2ks/wiki)). Multiple choice, the format that suits fast mobile combat, was the most effective single practice-test format ([WSU on Adesope et al.](https://news.wsu.edu/news/2018/01/09/educational-psychology-article-among-nations-most-read/)). Two design implications follow. The answer must never leak into monster flavour text. A wrong answer should cost a little HP, never XP or gold already earned, because the classroom effects came from low-stakes quizzes.

Spacing is where most of the remaining value lies. For a one-week horizon, the best gap between reviews is about 20–40% of the time until the test, falling to 5–10% for a year. A gap that is too long costs less than one that is too short ([Cepeda et al. 2008](https://www.evullab.org/pdf/CepedaVulRohrerWixtedPashler-PS-2008.pdf)). Duolingo's half-life regression model predicts recall as p = 2^(−days/half-life). It cut prediction error by more than 45%, and in a six-week A/B test with about a million learners one variant **raised next-day return by 12%** ([Settles & Meeder 2016](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174.pdf)). The same paper has a warning: a better scheduler *lowered* practice sessions by 7.3% in another arm, because accurate predictions made reviews feel less needed. Engagement and learning have to be measured separately. Quizlet's data shows learners won't come back for spacing on their own: **95% study a set over four days at most** ([Quizlet tech blog](https://medium.com/tech-quizlet/spaced-repetition-for-all-cognitive-science-meets-big-data-in-a-procrastinating-world-59e4d2c8ede1)). The game has to supply the reason to return. EDUVENTURE already has that reason in its daily missions and streaks, so the scheduler should fill those missions. When a concept's predicted recall drops below about 0.85, a "Returning" monster of that species appears on the player's usual route and drops bonus rewards. Beating it on time gives more than beating a new monster, which rewards spacing over cramming. Duolingo's skill "strength meters" show that visible decay works at scale ([Settles & Meeder](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174.pdf)). No study has tested the "Returning monster" framing itself, so that part is a design bet.

Interleaving tells EDUVENTURE where to mix questions. Mixing problem types helps overall (**g = 0.42**), but **hurts for vocabulary lists (g = −0.39)** ([Brunmair & Richter 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/)). In a classroom maths replication, interleaved practice scored **72% vs 38% (d = 1.05)** ([Rohrer et al. 2014](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13423-014-0588-3)). The design is to teach one type at a time on first visit, then mix types in bosses and "wild" zones. In the Code Academy that means confusable constructs such as `for` vs `while` and `==` vs `===`. In the Language Hall it means grammar decisions such as Malay affixes (*me-*, *ber-*, *di-*) rather than word lists. Interleaving lowers in-session accuracy, so mixed battles should pay higher XP to stop them feeling like failure.

Difficulty should be adaptive and slightly generous. An Elo system that treats each answer as a match between student and item is "simple, robust and effective". Its author defaults practice to **75% success**, because 50% "does not seem very encouraging" ([Pelánek](https://www.fi.muni.cz/~xpelanek/publications/CAE-elo.pdf)), and school users of one system preferred around 65% ([Pelánek 2015](https://www.fi.muni.cz/~xpelanek/publications/aied15.pdf)). The "85% rule" is a theoretical result for simple binary learning tasks, not school subjects ([Wilson et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6831579)). A sensible starting band is **75–80% for normal monsters, 85–90% for Returning monsters and young primary pupils, and 60–65% for bosses**, re-tuned from the last ten answers and then A/B tested. Elo ratings are known to be biased in some contexts ([PMC 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12784335)), so treat them as a starting model.

Feedback and worked examples complete the learning loop. Feedback averages **d = 0.48**, and the more information it carries the better it works. Praise and rewards alone have small effects ([Wisniewski et al. 2020](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087/pdf)). XP and gold are therefore engagement, not feedback. Every wrong answer needs a short explanation of why the chosen option was wrong. Prompting self-explanation gives **g = 0.55**, with generic prompts beating specific ones, although most studies used undergraduates ([Bisra et al., BPS summary](https://www.bps.org.uk/research-digest/self-explanation-powerful-learning-technique-according-meta-analysis-64-studies)). That suits the Code Academy: a worked example, then a version with lines blanked, then writing the code from scratch.

Game research adds a constraint on presentation. Games beat non-game instruction modestly (**g = 0.33**). **Simpler visuals were as effective or more effective**, and multiple sessions beat single ones ([Clark et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4748544); [Cooney Center](https://joanganzcooneycenter.org/?p=16779)). Games were not reliably more motivating than conventional teaching (d = 0.26, not significant), and they worked better alongside other instruction ([Wouters et al. 2013](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/a-meta-analysis-of-the-cognitive-and-motivational-effects-of-seri/)). Decorative "seductive details" hurt learning most in lessons under five minutes ([Learning and the Brain](https://www.learningandthebrain.com/blog/seductive-details-when-do-cool-stories-and-videos-interfere-with-learning/)), which describes every EDUVENTURE question. The 3D chibi world is the reward between questions. The question card should be plain and high-contrast, with particles and ambient animation dimmed.

Habit research supports small, forgiving daily goals. Habits took a median of **66 days** to become automatic (range 18–254), and **missing one day did not derail them**, although very inconsistent people never formed one ([UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2009/aug/how-long-does-it-take-form-habit); [BPS](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/how-form-habit)). The participants were adults. A streak kept alive by three questions or one Returning monster, with one free shield a week, fits this evidence. The research also rules out one popular idea. Growth-mindset interventions average **d = 0.05**, falling to 0.02 in high-quality studies, although effects may be larger for at-risk students ([Macnamara & Burgoyne 2023](https://Www.Gwern.net/doc/statistics/meta-analysis/2023-macnamara.pdf); [Tipton et al.](https://ecrhub.org/publications/why-meta-analyses-growth-mindset-and-other-interventions-should-follow-best-practices)). Don't build a mindset lesson or market one. Build error-friendly mechanics: a comeback bonus for fixing a past mistake, and no progress lost on a defeat.

## The knowledge pet should grow from learning and never suffer

Pets can raise effort, but only when they are designed the right way. The strongest result is the **protégé effect**. Students who believed they were teaching their own agent spent more time on learning activities and learned more, with the largest gains for **lower-achieving students**. They also acknowledged errors more readily, perhaps because the agent buffered the sting of failure ([Chase et al. 2009](https://purl.stanford.edu/zm369yx3532)). Taiwan's My-Pet-My-Quest ran for a semester. Children formed real attachment, bought medicine for their pets, and over time moved from story segments into learning tasks ([Liao et al. 2012](https://rptel.apsce.net/index.php/RPTEL/article/download/2012-07003/244)). That evidence is descriptive, not a randomised study. Generic on-screen pedagogical agents give only a small effect, about g = 0.20 ([Castro-Alonso et al. via X-MOL](https://www.x-mol.com/paper/1346212315536674816)). The pet's value is therefore motivational and relational, not instructional, and a pet that only "eats points" misses the stronger protégé mechanism.

Finch, a self-care app, is the commercial model to copy. Its bird grows from real-world goal completion, goes on timed adventures, sleeps at night and is auto-fed, "so there's no grieving" ([Her Campus](https://www.hercampus.com/?p=955862)). Reported **D1/D7 retention of 54%/37%** edges past Duolingo's 51%/35% (Sensor Tower figures via [Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/x0hd2ssr80y5n7gv0w967pg7hwd7tl)). Reviews centre on attachment. The worst ones describe *losing* invested progress, such as a **234-day streak reset after two missed days** ([Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/)). The pet's +3 per streak day should therefore become +3 per *active day*, so a lapse never costs the pet anything. The failure modes are well documented. Tamagotchi deaths left children grieving and guilty, and in 1997 schools in China, Hong Kong, Thailand and the US banned the toy because it couldn't be paused ([Strong Museum](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/tamagotchi/)). An academic analysis framed by children's rights classes Duolingo's "excessive notifications and emotionally charged visuals" as manipulative ([deceptive.design](https://deceptive.design/articles/teaching-or-manipulating-on-the-adoption-of-bright-and-deceptive-patterns-by-duolingo)). Malaysian netizens compared the owl's nudges to **"toxic ex texts"** ([The Sun](https://thesun.my/going-viral/netizens-say-duolingo-notifications-feel-like-toxic-ex-texts)). The rule for EDUVENTURE: when the child is away, the pet naps or goes on an adventure, then greets them happily with a souvenir or a fun fact. It never sickens, runs away, de-evolves or begs. When the child answers wrongly, the pet fails *with* them ("we'll get it next time").

Evolution design should borrow Pokémon's branching but not Digimon's hidden rules. Pokémon layers level, items, friendship and conditions on branched families such as Eevee's eight forms ([Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Methods_of_Evolution)). Digimon World makes forms depend on hidden care-mistake counts, and players openly worry about getting the "bad" form ([r/digimon](https://red.bismuth.it/r/digimon/comments/1c4k6um/how_to_avoid_digivolving_into_numemon_digimon/kzom9cy/?context=3)). The design below meets the owner's request that evolution depend on learning, level *and* gold:

| Stage | Learning points (pet's own) | Player level | Gold | What the child sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Egg → baby | Chosen at level 2 (kancil, beruang or naga) | 2 | None | Hatching scene |
| Baby → young | 100 | e.g. 5 | Token "Upacara Evolusi" fee, below one day's typical learning earnings | New model, trick and idle animation; micro-milestones at 50 points |
| Young → adult | 300, tracked per island subject | e.g. 12 | Token ceremony fee; an optional deluxe ceremony (fireworks, frame, title) as a gold sink | **Form chosen by the dominant subject** (e.g. a Science, Maths, Technology or Language naga), with a rare "Seimbang" form for a balanced mix; micro-milestone at 200 |
| After adult | Continues | Later | Cosmetics only | Re-specialisation scroll earned through learning, not bought |

The level thresholds and form names are illustrative. Four rules keep the table honest. First, **learning points and level are the real gates. Gold works only as a token, because gold must be earn-only.** If gold could ever be bought, a gold gate would let money buy around learning, which is exactly the Prodigy pattern of "better pets" for members that critics singled out ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/prodigy-math-game-ftc-complaint-d693af67-019a-4ff5-9bff-8c73d1277342.html)). Second, weight the +1 per correct answer by difficulty and novelty, and cap points from repeated easy items each day. Otherwise the pet rewards farming. Third, show a visible meter for each subject branch so the child chooses deliberately, and make every form equally desirable. Fourth, frame points as the pet *learning from you*, not as payment. Deci's data favour informational framing over large expected payouts ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10589297/)).

Gold customization is the right outlet for the owner's second request. Practitioners agree the healthiest gold sinks are "optional but desirable (cosmetic mounts, housing)", with housing the strongest because it keeps absorbing decoration spending ([Medium: Designing Game Economies](https://medium.com/@msahinn21/designing-game-economies-inflation-resource-management-and-balance-fa1e6c894670)). Habitica's pets give no gameplay bonus, yet collecting them is a core long-term goal ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Website/Habitica)). EDUVENTURE's pet shop should sell accessories, colours, habitat decorations, festival outfits, emotes, photo frames and name-change scrolls. Prices should be fixed, with no randomness and no stat boosts. Cultural range matters: Finch drew negative reviews for limited textured-hair options ([Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/)), so include baju kurung, songkok-style accessories, and Raya, CNY, Deepavali, Gawai and Kaamatan sets from the start. Any gameplay help should take the shape of learning. Examples are one "Petua" hint per quiz that rephrases the concept without giving the answer, and a protégé moment where the pet "attempts" a question and the child corrects it. Never let a bought item make the pet answer questions, and never give the pet stat boosts in competitive modes.

## Co-op beats ranking, and the class is the safest social unit

Cooperation has the deepest evidence base of any social mechanic. Across 122 studies, cooperative goal structures beat competitive ones by **about 0.78 SD**. **Cooperation combined with competition between groups beat individual work by 0.50**, while competition between individuals did no better than working alone ([Johnson & Maruyama 1981 summary](https://maaw.info/ArticleSummaries/ArtSumJohnsonMaruyama1981.htm)). A later series of 231 studies found g = 0.42 for cooperative over competitive work ([UMN Conservancy](https://conservancy.umn.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/d484edf9-acfb-418e-ae59-010722bfce7a/content)). This maps directly onto **class-vs-class or clan-vs-clan raids**. Every member's correct answers damage a shared boss, and each child's contribution appears only as a positive ("+12 hits"), never as a bottom rank.

Leaderboards motivate some players and hurt others. Duolingo's league gains came mostly from teens and adults. In a classroom, a male-dominated leaderboard changed women's maths scores, which shows that board composition alone triggers stereotype effects ([Christy & Fox 2014](https://dblp1.uni-trier.de/rec/journals/ce/ChristyF14.html)). Absolute boards motivated only the top third, while **relative boards motivated and engaged all learners equally** ([HKU repository](https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/368255/1/content.pdf)). Relative feedback helped low-achieving primary pupils ([Mainz discussion paper](https://download.uni-mainz.de/RePEc/pdf/Discussion_Paper_2111.pdf)). The safe version is opt-in, uses small cohorts matched on last week's effort, ranks by capped learning XP rather than test scores, shows only nearby ranks, has no demotion for under-13s, and shows nicknames without gender, school or location.

Friend streaks carry a regulatory flag. Learners with a Duolingo Friend Streak were **22% more likely to finish their daily lesson**. That figure is correlational, and the feature is capped at five friends ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-lessons-friend-streak/)). Snapchat's streaks show where this ends up: in one survey of people aged 13–21, a quarter had paid to restore a broken streak (secondary summary, [pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/consumer-tech/blogs/new-study-reveals-how-young-people-are-influenced-by-gamification-features-on-snapchat-386b9eae-9eb9-4fb1-8d38-08092e9e2791)). The EU now says **communication streaks should be off by default for minors** ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-guidelines-protection-minors)). EDUVENTURE's version should be a weekly shared *learning* goal among up to five code-linked friends. It pauses rather than resets, has no paid restore, and sends no "your streak is dying" alerts.

Teachers are both the growth channel and the safety channel. Prodigy grew through free teacher accounts to almost 500,000 teachers ([Coda analysis](https://coda.io/@nila/competitive-analysis/prodigy-education-2)), and a rostering integration took it to more than 8,300 districts in a year ([Clever](https://www.clever.com/?p=12110)). Minecraft Education keeps multiplayer inside a school tenant. Players join with a picture code, face a lockout after five failed attempts, and games are capped at 30 players ([Minecraft Education support](https://edusupport.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360047555531)). A class-only instance means an under-13 player mostly meets known classmates. Parent features have weaker retention evidence. A review of 29 studies found school-to-parent messages help attainment **only when they are two-way and personalised** ([Durham](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/1243648)). A parent account is still essential, because it is where consent, friend approval and time limits live. Reports should be specific ("mastered fractions; ask her about X"), and parents should be able to reply with encouragement.

Child-safety practice has converged on clear rules. Toontown's SpeedChat used a finite menu of phrases, so "it is impossible to communicate any personal information" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/postmortem-disney-online-s-toontown)). Free chat was allowed only with real-life friends who exchanged codes. Roblox's 2025–26 changes, driven by at least 35 lawsuits, are the current reference point. They require age checks before any chat, chat only within similar age bands, chat off by default for under-9s, and no photo or video sharing ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/18/tech/roblox-ai-age-verification-youth-safety)). Teens can add adults only through real-world channels such as an in-person QR scan ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/07/advancing-safety-on-roblox-with-age-based-communication)). State settlements added restrictions on adult-to-minor contact (Alabama, $12.2M) ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/conormurray/2026/04/21/roblox-settles-with-alabama-over-child-safety-concerns-but-still-faces-state-lawsuits/)) and an alert the first time a minor enters a private chat ([WV Attorney General](https://ago.wv.gov/article/west-virginia-reaches-11-million-settlement-roblox-prompts-major-child-safety-overhaul)). Grooming typically moves the child to private apps ("off-linking") ([STV News](https://news.stv.tv/world/its-where-predators-get-children-mums-roblox-warning-over-grooming-risks)), and offenders build trust with gifts ([CEOP](https://www.ceopeducation.co.uk/parents/articles/gaming)). EDUVENTURE's three age bands already follow the Toontown pattern. They need five additions: no chat between 18+ and under-18 accounts without a verified link; a separate shard for university players; filters that block numbers, URLs and other-app handles; no gifting or trading between unconnected players; and no image uploads. Share cards should be static images with the chibi avatar and aggregate stats, without names, school, location or friend codes. For under-13s, sharing should go through the parent ([Android Authority on Duolingo's card](https://www.androidauthority.com/duolingo-year-in-review-2025-3621782)).

## Regulators now name streaks, loot boxes and pet guilt explicitly

The design limits now come from four regulatory directions plus the app stores, and they agree with each other. A JAMA study found manipulative design in roughly **80% of apps played by children aged 3–5**. It named four patterns: parasocial pressure (a character urging the child to play or buy), fabricated time pressure, navigation constraints and lures ([Radesky et al. 2022](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2793493)). EU guidance lists **streaks, attention-recapturing notifications, countdown timers, "buy now" prompts, intermediate virtual currencies and loot boxes** ([Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/european-commission-issues-guidelines-on-the-protection-of-minors)). Each maps onto an EDUVENTURE system: streaks, the pet, missions, notifications and gold.

A few judgement calls follow from the table. A wide age range plus chat makes **Apple's general listing with an honest age rating** a better fit than the Kids Category, although Guideline 5.1.4 still applies to minors' data. Brazil has a conflicting description of its rule, Malaysia's DPO and breach-notification figures were not verified against primary sources, and nothing here has been reviewed by counsel. The direction is still unambiguous. **Gold that can only be earned keeps the whole shop outside virtual-currency and loot-box rules.** Notifications that are off by default for minors, controlled by parents and silent at night and during school hours satisfy the UK, the EU and the FTC's stated concern at once. A pet that never suffers removes the "simulated relationship" pressure the DFA targets.

## Safety, the syllabus and festivals give EDUVENTURE a Malaysian advantage

Malaysia's exam reforms left a gap that a game can fill. UPSR was abolished in 2021 and PT3 in 2022. They were replaced by school-based PBD mastery levels (TP1–TP6) and a school-run UASA end-of-year test ([Borneo Post](https://www.theborneopost.com/2024/11/11/upsr-pt3-no-longer-relevant-moe-to-continue-strengthen-school-based-assessment-system-says-minister/)). Teachers and academics still dispute the change ([FMT](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2024/11/14/wheres-study-on-impact-of-removing-upsr-pt3-exams-academic-asks-govt)). SPM, with **413,299 candidates** in 2025, is the only remaining national high-stakes exam ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/03/31/fewer-top-scorers-in-spm-2025-despite-overall-improvement-in-passes)). Parents have lost their national benchmark. A report saying "your child is at TP4 in Sains topic X" replaces it, and doubles as the parent-side reason to keep a child playing. KP2027 starts with Year 1 and Form 1 in 2027 and brings basic AI into primary schools ([Berita Harian](https://www.bharian.com.my/amp/berita/nasional/2026/10/1690448/asas-ai-diperkenal-di-sekolah-rendah-mulai-2027-fadhlina)). Tagging every question by curriculum version, year and TP level now makes that switch a content swap, and the Code Academy lines up with "Asas AI".

The teacher channel is open but under-used. In 2026, DELIMa active-user rates were **97.73% for teachers but only 37.32% for students** ([Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2584910)). The ministry recommended Quizizz and Kahoot during online teaching ([MDPI 2021](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/sustainability/sustainability-13-06436/article_deploy/sustainability-13-06436-v4.pdf)), but interview research says gamification is demanding for teachers ([HRMARS 2025](https://hrmars.com/papers_submitted/24510/assessing-teachers-readiness-for-integrating-gamification-in-esl-instruction-using-quizizz-in-teaching-vocabulary-skills.pdf)). A zero-prep "pick a topic and TP level, get a class quest link" mode fits that gap. Since **1 June 2026 under-16s cannot hold accounts on major social platforms** ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/06/01/malaysias-under-16-social-media-rule-starts-today-what-parents-need-to-know/221674)), so EDUVENTURE cannot reach most students through TikTok. It has to grow teacher-first and parent-first. Teacher creators such as Sir Fathi, with 721.5k TikTok followers ([Sinar Daily](https://www.sinardaily.my/article/736174/focus/national/15-malaysian-teacher-influencers-making-waves-on-social-media)), and MDEC's Cikgu Juara Digital network are natural partners. SPM-age students aged 16 and over are the one cohort reachable directly.

In Malaysia, safety is a selling point rather than a cost. In late 2025 the government considered banning Roblox. The Communications Minister said he had banned his own children from playing it, and the government ultimately chose tighter moderation over a ban ([Lowyat](https://www.lowyat.net/2025/371894/govt-considering-possible-ban-on-roblox-over-child-safety-concerns/); [Newswav](https://newswav.com/article/no-roblox-ban-but-safety-tightened-A2512_BmNUtc)). Only 34.4% of parents used parental controls ([The Star](https://thestar.com.my/news/nation/2023/02/22/make-cyberspace-safer-for-kids)), so controls built into the game do work that parents currently don't. "The safe Roblox-like world that follows the KSSR" is a positioning no local competitor holds. Pandai offers quizzes and notes to about a million self-reported users ([BFM](https://www.bfm.my/content/podcast/pandai-from-y-combinator-to-rm16m-funding-and-1m-users)), EduNation offers videos, and Upin & Ipin Universe is a culturally Malaysian open world that is neither curriculum-based nor on mobile ([Bernama](https://mrem.bernama.com/viewsm.php?idm=51500)).

Culture and language are the stickiness layer. MLBB's Badang, a hero drawn from Malay folklore, and its 10th-anniversary carnival in Kuching show that local myth and community events drive play ([Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/mlbb-turns-10-in-malaysia-with-a-133-hero-parade-in-kuching)). Upin & Ipin's 13B+ YouTube views show the reach of Malaysian kids' content ([Marketing Magazine](https://marketingmagazine.com.my/?p=133919)). EDUVENTURE's three eggs already carry that resonance: Sang Kancil the trickster, the beruang madu, the naga. Festival events for Raya, CNY, Deepavali, Gawai, Kaamatan, Merdeka and Hari Malaysia supply a ready-made live-ops calendar. Those events must be inclusive, carry no penalty for missing them, and never turn religious ritual into a game mechanic. Language policy is a second advantage. Under the Dual Language Programme, schools teach Science and Maths in English or BM, and new guidelines apply to all government schools from July 2026 ([The Sun](https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/all-govt-schools-must-adhere-to-the-new-dlp-guidelines-education-minister-lp12597693/)). A per-subject BM/English toggle that mirrors the child's school is something English-only global apps can't offer. Mobile data is not the constraint: Malaysia was the cheapest of 34 markets in 2026 ([Giraffy](https://giraffy.com/research/mobile-data-prices-2026)). Low-end Android performance is the likelier constraint once the mobile apps arrive. For worldwide expansion, the islands become a "home region" and each country adds a curriculum pack. Brunei and Indonesia share language and folklore, following Ruangguru's regional path ([KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/indonesian-edtech-startup-ruangguru-takes-its-classes-to-vietnam)). Byju's collapse from a US$22B valuation is the warning against hard-sell marketing to parents ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/from-22-billion-peak-to-debt-traps-and-lawsuits-how-byjus-empire-collapsed-533611-2026-05-28)).

### Build next

**1. Learning-weighted rewards and a learning-first North Star metric.** *Evidence:* CURR moved DAU 5x more than any other lever, and Duolingo's later XP re-weighting added 1.1–1.8M learning minutes a day after grinding distorted its leagues ([Lenny's](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth); [Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/time-spent-learning-well)). *Ethical risk:* low. The metric is the guardrail against Prodigy's ratio of 16 ads to 4 problems. Track and publish questions answered per minute played. *Effort:* S. *Maps onto:* XP, gold and pet points scale with question difficulty and novelty, with a daily cap on repeated easy items. The dashboard tracks next-day return of current players next to curriculum minutes at target difficulty, aiming for Khan Academy's 30 minutes a week ([Khan](https://2023-2024.annualreport.khanacademy.org/efficacy-results)).

**2. Elo-tuned battles with curriculum-tagged questions.** *Evidence:* Elo is simple and robust, and its author defaults practice to 75% success ([Pelánek](https://www.fi.muni.cz/~xpelanek/publications/CAE-elo.pdf)). Mastery structure, not badges, carries Khan's effects. *Ethical risk:* low. Watch fairness for second-language learners, and offer an accuracy-first mode with no speed scoring, given Kahoot's time-pressure harms ([Wang & Tahir](https://research.gold.ac.uk/id/eprint/39435)). *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* every server-marked answer updates the question's rating and the player's per-skill rating. The admin review workflow gains required tags for skill, curriculum (KSSR, KSSM or KP2027), year and TP level, plus a starting difficulty. Monsters target 75–80% success, Returning monsters and young primary pupils 85–90%, and bosses 60–65%.

**3. Returning monsters: a spaced-repetition scheduler inside daily missions.** *Evidence:* spacing is rated high utility, and a half-life model raised next-day return by 12% in one A/B arm ([Settles & Meeder](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174.pdf)). Learners won't space their study on their own ([Quizlet](https://medium.com/tech-quizlet/spaced-repetition-for-all-cognitive-science-meets-big-data-in-a-procrastinating-world-59e4d2c8ede1)). *Ethical risk:* low. Decay must look gentle, with monsters returning and no punishment, and learning and engagement should be A/B tested separately because of the −7.3% practice result. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* store a half-life per player per concept on the server. When predicted recall falls below about 0.85, spawn a Returning monster of that species on the player's island route and fill daily mission slots such as "Defeat 5 Returning monsters + explore 1 new area". Wrong answers shorten the half-life.

**4. Insight cards after wrong answers.** *Evidence:* feedback averages d = 0.48, information-rich feedback works best, and rewards alone barely help ([Wisniewski et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087/pdf)). *Ethical risk:* low. *Effort:* S to build, with continuing content work. *Maps onto:* show the right/wrong signal instantly in combat, then a skippable one- or two-line card explaining why the chosen option was wrong. The admin review workflow requires an explanation for each distractor before a question goes live. Code items name the error type and the line, and Language Hall items show the rule plus a contrasting example.

**5. Forgiving streaks with earned shields.** *Evidence:* separating the goal from the streak gave +3.3% D14; protection gave +4% one-week return; a commitment wager gave +14% D7 ([Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak); [Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals)). An RCT found streaks raised maths achievement ([NBER](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34173)). A single missed day doesn't break a habit ([UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2009/aug/how-long-does-it-take-form-habit)). *Ethical risk:* medium. Streaks are named by the EU and the DFA proposal, so players must be able to switch them off, shields apply automatically, there is no loss animation or loss notification, and shields are never sold. *Effort:* S. *Maps onto:* the existing streak counts a tiny minimum (three questions or one Returning monster). One shield a week is earned through play. Under-13 accounts can use a "weekly active days" view. An occasional revival event works as an amnesty. The pet's +3 per streak day becomes +3 per active day.

**6. Knowledge pet v1: grows from learning and level, gold as a token, never suffers.** *Evidence:* the protégé effect raised effort, most for low achievers ([Chase et al.](https://purl.stanford.edu/zm369yx3532)). Finch's no-punishment pet reports D1/D7 of 54%/37% ([Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/x0hd2ssr80y5n7gv0w967pg7hwd7tl)). Gated "better pets" were central to the Prodigy complaint ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/prodigy-math-game-ftc-complaint-d693af67-019a-4ff5-9bff-8c73d1277342.html)). *Ethical risk:* medium. This is parasocial pressure if done badly, so there is no sadness, sickness, death, running away or de-evolution, notifications are curiosity-framed and off by default, and setbacks are shared supportively. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* the egg choice at level 2 (kancil, beruang or naga). Young needs 100 points plus a level gate, and adult needs 300 points plus a higher level gate, each with a token "Upacara Evolusi" gold fee. Micro-milestones at 50 and 200 points. Fixed-price gold accessories and colours. The pet follows the hero.

**7. Earn-only gold and a fixed-price cosmetic shop.** *Evidence:* Google requires game coins to be clearly distinct from real money ([Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335)). The EU advises against intermediate currencies ([Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/european-commission-issues-guidelines-on-the-protection-of-minors)). Healthy sinks are optional and desirable ([Medium](https://medium.com/@msahinn21/designing-game-economies-inflation-resource-management-and-balance-fa1e6c894670)). *Ethical risk:* low once gold is earn-only. That single decision removes most loot-box and currency-obfuscation risk. *Effort:* S, mostly a policy decision plus a faucet-and-sink model. *Maps onto:* gold and the shop. Shop tiers unlock by level and sell cosmetics and comfort items, never stats or learning skips. Any free random drop shows its possible contents and avoids spinning or near-miss animations.

**8. Age-banded safety baseline and parental consent.** *Evidence:* Epic paid $520M partly over default-on chat ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/12/fortnite-video-game-maker-epic-games-pay-more-half-billion-dollars-over-ftc-allegations)). Roblox's 2025–26 reforms ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/18/tech/roblox-ai-age-verification-youth-safety)). Malaysia's Child Protection Code ([Skrine](https://www.skrine.com/insights/alerts/may-2026/online-safety-act-2025-child-protection-code-and-r)). PDPA consent for under-18s via the parent or guardian. *Ethical risk:* this feature *is* the mitigation. The residual risk is over-collecting data for age checks, so minimise it. *Effort:* L. *Maps onto:* the three age bands. Use a neutral age screen at sign-up and parent consent for under-18 Malaysian accounts. Under-13s get preset chat only, with friends by code. 13–17 chat is filtered and off until enabled. Accounts aged 18+ go on a separate shard with no contact with minors without a verified link. Filters block numbers, URLs and other-app handles. No gifting or trading between unconnected players, and no image uploads. Report and block on every avatar, with reports acknowledged within an hour.

**9. Wellbeing defaults: notifications, quiet hours and breaks.** *Evidence:* ICO Standard 5 on "sticky" features ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/5-detrimental-use-of-data/)), the FTC's stated concern about push notifications ([Jones Day](https://jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/05/ftc-finalizes-amendments-to-coppa--rule)), and Tamagotchi school bans ([Strong Museum](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/tamagotchi/)). *Ethical risk:* low. The cost is some lost reactivation, which is acceptable. *Effort:* S. *Maps onto:* notifications are opt-in and parent-controlled for minors, with no messages at night or during school hours. Mission sessions run 5–15 minutes with a "great stopping point" summary. A break reminder appears after a long session, and parents can set time limits.

### Build soon

**10. Mastery-gated, interleaved bosses with training camps.** *Evidence:* interleaving g = 0.42 and d = 1.05 in a classroom ([Brunmair & Richter](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/); [Rohrer et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13423-014-0588-3)). The Khan mastery RCT showed +0.12–0.22 SD (via [Khan blog](https://blog.khanacademy.org/multiple-studies-show-khan-academy-drives-learning-gains-evidence-for-our-platforms-effectiveness)). *Ethical risk:* low to medium, because harder bosses can frustrate. Lost bosses still give a small reward and never cost progress. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* keep the level gate and add a mastery gate shown as an "Island Crest" bar. Boss phases mix that island's problem types. A failed boss creates a short training camp on the exact skills that failed before a rematch with fresh questions. A comeback bonus rewards fixing past mistakes.

**11. Class raids and clan co-op.** *Evidence:* cooperation beats competition (about 0.78), and cooperation with competition between groups beats individual work (0.50) ([Johnson & Maruyama](https://maaw.info/ArticleSummaries/ArtSumJohnsonMaruyama1981.htm)). Social interaction moderates gamification effects ([Sailer & Homner](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/frontdoor/index/index/docId/109056)). *Ethical risk:* medium. Avoid group penalties of the Forest kind, where one child's lapse hurts everyone ([Make Headway](https://makeheadway.com/blog/forest-app-review/)). Show contributions only as positives. *Effort:* L. *Maps onto:* a shared boss that every member damages with correct answers, weekly clan-vs-clan or class-vs-class totals, preset-chat clan channels for under-13s, and clans that are class-scoped or approved by a teacher or parent.

**12. Teacher mode with class instances and zero-prep quests.** *Evidence:* Prodigy's free-teacher growth ([Clever](https://www.clever.com/?p=12110)), Minecraft Education's tenant-scoped worlds ([Minecraft Education](https://edusupport.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360047555531)), games working better alongside instruction ([Wouters](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/a-meta-analysis-of-the-cognitive-and-motivational-effects-of-seri/)), and DELIMa student activity at only 37% ([Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2584910)). *Ethical risk:* medium. The Edmodo precedent means school-authorised data is used only for education, never for ads ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/05/ftc-says-ed-tech-provider-edmodo-unlawfully-used-childrens-personal-information-advertising-outsourced-compliance-school)). *Effort:* L. *Maps onto:* free class codes and a class-only island instance. A "pick topic + TP level" quest generator draws on admin-reviewed questions. A five-minute retrieval "class raid" can open a lesson. Teachers get class and skill reports, can hide boards, and can share links through Google Classroom or Teams (DELIMa SSO not verified).

**13. Parent account and TP Tracker report.** *Evidence:* parent messages help only when two-way and personalised ([Durham](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/1243648)). Parents lost UPSR and PT3 benchmarks ([Borneo Post](https://www.theborneopost.com/2024/11/11/upsr-pt3-no-longer-relevant-moe-to-continue-strengthen-school-based-assessment-system-says-minister/)). *Ethical risk:* medium, because of surveillance. Tell the child when a parent is monitoring (ICO Standard 11), and keep reports to learning data. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* the parent account is the consent hub for the under-13 and 13–17 bands, with friend approval, chat and time controls. The weekly report gives TP level per topic and is easy to forward on WhatsApp. Parents can send in-game encouragement stickers.

**14. Pet branching evolution, adventures and "teach your pet".** *Evidence:* protégé effect ([Chase et al.](https://purl.stanford.edu/zm369yx3532)), Finch's adventures as "anti-nag" hooks ([Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/x0hd2ssr80y5n7gv0w967pg7hwd7tl)), and Eevee-style branching ([Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pokémon_with_branched_evolutions)). No study has measured branching's effect on attachment. *Ethical risk:* low if branches are visible and equally desirable, which avoids Digimon's "bad form" anxiety. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* each pet tracks points per island subject, and at adulthood the dominant subject sets the form, with a rare balanced form. After N lessons the pet goes on an adventure and brings back a souvenir or a Malaysian fun fact. Occasionally the pet "tries" a question and the child corrects it.

**15. Code Academy fading scrolls and Language Hall grammar duels.** *Evidence:* self-explanation g = 0.55 ([BPS](https://www.bps.org.uk/research-digest/self-explanation-powerful-learning-technique-according-meta-analysis-64-studies)). Interleaving helps confusable categories but not word lists ([Brunmair & Richter](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/)). *Ethical risk:* low. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* in the Code Academy, a worked example with a "why" question, then fill in the blanked lines, then write from scratch, with the first step skipped as Elo rises. Add "Debug the Bug" and "predict the output" monsters, plus a bilingual Python/JavaScript bug monster. In the Language Hall, run interleaved duels on confusable grammar (affixes, *di* vs *di-*, English tenses), keep vocabulary blocked, and use lighter picture-based prompts for primary pupils.

**16. BM/English toggle per subject.** *Evidence:* the DLP lets schools choose English or BM for Science and Maths, and new guidelines apply to all government schools ([The Sun](https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/all-govt-schools-must-adhere-to-the-new-dlp-guidelines-education-minister-lp12597693/)). *Ethical risk:* low. Translation errors would undermine trust, so route translations through admin review. *Effort:* L, mostly content. *Maps onto:* every Science, Maths and Technology question exists in both languages, set per subject to match the child's school. Light Manglish NPC banter stays out of graded Language Hall content.

**17. Malaysian festival and school-calendar events.** *Evidence:* Badang and MLBB's local events ([MOONTON](https://en.moonton.com/news/371.html)); Upin & Ipin's reach ([Marketing Magazine](https://marketingmagazine.com.my/?p=133919)). No data exists on festival-event lift in Malaysian edtech. *Ethical risk:* medium. Fear of missing out and religious sensitivity are the risks, so use long windows, keep event rewards cosmetic, carry no penalty for missing, celebrate every community, and never make ritual a mechanic. *Effort:* M per season once the framework exists. *Maps onto:* themed daily missions and Sejarah quizzes for Merdeka and Hari Malaysia. Kolam puzzles, ketupat and congkak minigames placed on the matching subject islands. Festival outfits in the gold shop.

**18. Opt-in matched effort leagues, 13+ first.** *Evidence:* +17% learning time and 3x highly engaged learners ([Lenny's](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)), weighed against Hanus & Fox's harm in class settings and the finding that relative boards motivate everyone ([HKU](https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/368255/1/content.pdf)). *Ethical risk:* medium. Social comparison and stereotype threat are the risks, so leagues are opt-in, effort-ranked with capped XP, show only nearby ranks, use nicknames only, and have no demotion for under-13s. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* weekly cohorts of about 20–30 matched on the previous week's learning XP. Titles serve as cosmetic league rewards, alongside a personal-best board. For under-13s, a "most improved" class board comes first.

### Later

**19. Weekly friend quests.** *Evidence:* Friend Streak users were 22% more likely to finish daily lessons, a correlational figure ([Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-lessons-friend-streak/)). *Ethical risk:* medium. The EU names communication streaks, so make quests weekly and about learning, cap them at five friends, pause rather than reset, and never sell restores. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* friends by code and daily missions, with a shared weekly progress bar.

**20. Pet habitat, photo mode, emotes and safe share cards.** *Evidence:* housing is the strongest gold sink ([Massively OP](https://massivelyop.com/2015/09/13/mmo-mechanics-economic-stagnation-in-mmo-economies/)). Finch spread through user content on TikTok ([Her Campus](https://www.hercampus.com/?p=955862)). *Ethical risk:* medium for sharing, so cards are image-only and anonymous, and under-13s share via a parent, in line with the under-16 social media rule. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* gold shop sinks, pet emotes such as zapin or joget, evolution cards, and class "Wrapped" cards ("Class 5 Amanah defeated 40 bosses").

**21. SPM season mode and exam-aware spacing.** *Evidence:* 413,299 SPM candidates ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/03/31/fewer-top-scorers-in-spm-2025-despite-overall-improvement-in-passes)), and the optimal spacing gap shrinks as the test approaches ([Cepeda et al.](https://www.evullab.org/pdf/CepedaVulRohrerWixtedPashler-PS-2008.pdf)). *Ethical risk:* medium, because exam stress means no countdown pressure in the UI. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* a focus mode that hides cosmetic distractions, a higher recall target (about 0.9) in the scheduler, and spacing gaps set from an exam date the student enters.

**22. Mobile age signals and a lite mode for low-end Android.** *Evidence:* Apple's Declared Age Range and Google's Play Age Signals APIs ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1720916/countdown-to-jan-1-2026-mobile-developers-must-adopt-apple-google-apis-to-comply-with-app-store-accountability-acts)), and Malaysian children are mostly on phones ([UNICEF Malaysia](https://www.unicef.org/malaysia/media/1506/file)). *Ethical risk:* low. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* the Android and iOS launch, with age signals setting the age-band defaults and a low-poly or 2D fallback client.

**23. Mandarin and Tamil UI, plus KP2027 and Asas AI content.** *Evidence:* KP2027 starts in 2027 with basic AI at primary level ([Berita Harian](https://www.bharian.com.my/amp/berita/nasional/2026/10/1690448/asas-ai-diperkenal-di-sekolah-rendah-mulai-2027-fadhlina)), and Tamil-medium teachers already use gamified tools ([UM Tamil Peraivu](https://ijps.um.edu.my/index.php/tamilperaivu/article/download/56230/17571/154378)). *Ethical risk:* low. *Effort:* L. *Maps onto:* UI and parent reports for SJKC and SJKT families. A Sang Kancil logic and AI quest line in Technology City. Curriculum tags from feature 2 make the content swap possible.

**24. Parent-gated cosmetic purchases or a supporter subscription, only if needed.** *Evidence:* ABCmouse's $10M settlement over hard cancellation ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2020/09/childrens-online-learning-program-abcmouse-pay-10-million-settle-ftc-charges-illegal-marketing-billing-practices)), and Apple's parental-gate rules ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). *Ethical risk:* high if it reaches children, so allow purchases only through a parental gate, shown in ringgit, cosmetic only, kept separate from gold, with one-step cancellation and no prompts in the child's gameplay. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* the shop, plus a school licence for teacher dashboards as the alternative revenue line.

**25. Inter-school "Piala EDUVENTURE" league.** *Evidence:* cooperation combined with competition between groups ([Johnson & Maruyama](https://maaw.info/ArticleSummaries/ArtSumJohnsonMaruyama1981.htm)), and MLBB's community events ([Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/mlbb-turns-10-in-malaysia-with-a-133-hero-parade-in-kuching)). *Ethical risk:* medium. Show school-level results only, never individual children, and the league needs JPN or MOE endorsement. *Effort:* M. *Maps onto:* class raids plus teacher mode, timed to Merdeka.

### Do not build

| Mechanic | Why not | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Paid loot boxes, gacha, or random items bought with any purchasable currency | Banned for minors in Brazil; PEGI 16 and Australian M ratings; FTC Genshin order ([Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/en/unico/brazils-eca-digital/); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/genshin-impact-game-developer-will-be-banned-selling-lootboxes-teens-under-16-without-parental-consent-pay-20-million-fine)) | Fixed-price cosmetics; free drops with visible contents |
| Energy or hearts that block learning until the player pays or watches an ad | Pay-to-learn friction; Duolingo backlash ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/)) | HP loss inside a battle only, never a lockout |
| Pay-to-skip timers, chest slots that expire, "buy now" countdowns | Named by EU guidance and FTC dark-pattern work ([Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/european-commission-issues-guidelines-on-the-protection-of-minors)) | Long-window events with cosmetic rewards |
| Pet evolution that gold can force, or any paid pet progress | Lets money skip learning; the Prodigy pattern ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/prodigy-math-game-ftc-complaint-d693af67-019a-4ff5-9bff-8c73d1277342.html)) | Learning + level gates with a token earn-only gold fee |
| A pet that dies, sickens, runs away, de-evolves or looks sad when the child is away | Parasocial pressure; Tamagotchi grief ([Radesky](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2793493); [Wellcome](https://wellcomecollection.org/stories/digital-pets)) | The pet naps, adventures and greets the child happily |
| Guilt notifications ("Your kancil misses you", "quitter") | Children take them personally; seen as manipulative ([deceptive.design](https://deceptive.design/articles/teaching-or-manipulating-on-the-adoption-of-bright-and-deceptive-patterns-by-duolingo)) | Opt-in, curiosity-framed, parent-controlled |
| Hard streak resets, paid streak restores, streak-loss animations | Finch's worst reviews; Snapchat paid restores ([Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/)) | Auto shields, active-day counting, revival events |
| Group penalties where one child's absence hurts others | Loss plus social blame ([Make Headway](https://makeheadway.com/blog/forest-app-review/)) | Contributions that only add |

| Global public leaderboards for young children, or ranking by test score | Harmed motivation and exam scores in class ([UCLA review](https://www.smhp.psych.ucla.edu/pdfdocs/gamil.pdf)) | Class and clan boards; relative, opt-in effort leagues |

| Adult–minor DMs, free trading or gifting, image uploads | Grooming vectors; Roblox settlements ([CEOP](https://www.ceopeducation.co.uk/parents/articles/gaming)) | Preset chat; class-scoped social features |
| Third-party ad or analytics SDKs in children's flows | COPPA separate consent; Apple 1.3; Google Families | First-party analytics only |

## Conclusion

The learning research and the regulation point the same way. The mechanics regulators now target, such as paid randomness, guilt-driven companions, countdown pressure and hard-reset streaks, are the ones with the weakest learning evidence. The mechanics with the strongest evidence, retrieval, spacing, mastery gating and cooperation, are not regulated at all. EDUVENTURE does not have to trade engagement for safety. Its real risk is choosing the wrong metric. Duolingo needed years and a public course correction to stop rewarding XP farming. EDUVENTURE can avoid that by weighting XP, gold and pet growth toward learning and by publishing its questions-per-minute ratio. In Malaysia, safety and curriculum fit are the growth engine rather than a cost, because the Roblox debate, the under-16 rule and the loss of UPSR and PT3 created demand for a safe game parents can trust and teachers can use.

What remains uncertain matters. No children's educational MMORPG has published retention data. Duolingo's lifts come mostly from teens and adults. The pet evidence is descriptive, and the "Returning monster" and subject-branching designs have never been tested. The right stance is to build the "build next" core and then run A/B tests that track learning and engagement separately, with the ethical rules above as fixed guardrails that no test can override.
