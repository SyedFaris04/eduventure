# My island Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A private 32 × 32 island per player, reached from the boatman. Players place, turn, move and remove about 50 decorations built in code, paid for with harvested materials, with some unlocked by level. The server checks every rule.

**Architecture:**
- `content/island-items.json` is the single catalogue (id, category, cost, level, w, h, blocks).
- `scripts/gen-island-items.mjs` writes its seed migration.
- A schema migration adds `island_catalog`, `island_items`, the RPCs and the `island_item` unlock kind.
- Client: a new place `my_island` (map file, jetty entry, no room); shared footprint rules in `src/features/island/rules.ts`; models in `src/world3d/props/islandProps.ts`; batched rendering in `src/world3d/IslandItems.tsx`; the build UI in `src/features/island/BuildPanel.tsx`; tile taps routed while building.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-my-island-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Footprint:** an item at (x, y) with turn r covers [x, x + w′) × [y, y + h′), where w′, h′ = w, h for r = 0 or 2 and h, w for r = 1 or 3. The build area is x 4–27 and y 4–27. At most 400 items. Removing refunds 100%.
- **Server:** every RPC is `security definer set search_path = ''`. Placing and moving check the daily play limit; removing doesn't.
- **Level gates:** `level_unlocks` kind `island_item`, key = item id. No row means open from level 1. `src/game/unlocks.ts` includes the island rows, derived from the JSON.
- **Size limits:** the build panel fits 740 × 360. All new text is in 7 languages.

## Review Focus

- **A modified client:** placing over another item, outside the area, without enough materials, above its level, or past 400 items is refused. Live tests cover each.
- **Two placements at once with materials for only one:** the per-player advisory lock serialises them. Live test with `Promise.all`.
- **Walking into a just-placed house:** blocked at once. A unit test drives the sim with an item.
- **Removing after the play limit:** allowed, and the refund goes through.
- **The island never joining a multiplayer room or loading camp, regrowing or festival data:** unit tests on the place helpers.
