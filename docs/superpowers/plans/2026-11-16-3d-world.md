# 3D World and Animation Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the 2D Phaser world with a real 3D chibi Greenhaven. It has a Clash-style fixed camera, smooth blended animation, and one 3D hero that is used everywhere.

**Architecture:**
- Today's Phaser logic (movement, wander, prompts, respawn) moves into a plain `WorldSim` class with no rendering, so it can be unit-tested.
- A React Three Fiber scene in `src/world3d/` draws the `WorldSim` state each frame.
- The hero is a stripped KayKit rig (skeleton plus 8 animation clips, no meshes). Our own low-poly parts are attached to its bones, so every appearance option still works.
- Static pictures (Home, HUD, Shop, dialogue, battle panels, creator thumbnails) are snapshots from one shared offscreen renderer.

**Tech Stack:** three 0.187, @react-three/fiber 9, @react-three/drei 10, React 19, Zustand 5, Vitest 5, Playwright. Dev only: @gltf-transform/core and @gltf-transform/functions, for the one-off asset script.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-3d-world-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The React-side contract in `src/game/store.ts` keeps `stick`, `prompt`, `interactSeq`, `dialogue`, `battle`, `boss`, `player`, `monsters`, `defeated`. Fields may be added; none are removed.
- `src/game/movement.ts`, `map.ts`, `maps/greenhaven.ts` and their tests stay unchanged.
- Only CC0 or self-made art. Every downloaded file is listed in `docs/art-credits.md` with source URL and licence.
- Model files are compressed GLB under `public/assets/models/`, totalling 6 MB or less.
- The 3D world loads as an on-demand chunk. `three` must not appear in the entry chunk.
- Performance targets:
  - 30 fps or more on a mid-range Android phone, 60 fps on desktop.
  - Device pixel ratio capped at 2 (1.5 on low quality).
  - One shadow-casting light, with shadows off on low quality.
  - Low quality switches on automatically when the frame time stays above 40 ms for 3 s.
- Reduced motion turns off camera easing, idle bob and sway, and makes one-shot animations jump to their end pose.
- No change to any server function, migration, battle rule or reward.
- All user-facing text goes through i18next, in all 7 locales.
- Standing rule (CLAUDE.md): work on branch `3d-world`. When unit, RLS, e2e, build and advisors are all green, merge to main, push and delete the branch.

## Review Focus

1. **No WebGL** (old phone, blocked GPU). The world page shows a translated "Your device can't show the 3D world" message instead of a blank screen. Tested in Task 7.
2. **Model download fails** (offline or flaky network). There's a translated error with a Retry button, not an endless spinner. Tested in Task 7.
3. **A battle opens.** The render loop stops (`frameloop="never"`) and restarts when the panel closes, as Phaser's `loop.sleep()` did. Tested in Task 6 through `WorldSim.paused`, and in Task 7.
4. **Rapid option clicks in the creator** must not leak GPU memory. Materials are shared per colour, and built parts are disposed when they're replaced. Tested in Task 3.
5. **Phone rotation or resize mid-game.** The camera aspect and `screenOf` stay correct, and taps still land where they're aimed. The e2e phone test runs at 360 × 740 after a resize.

**Files:**
- Create: `src/world3d/hero/parts.ts`, `src/world3d/hero/buildHero.ts`, `src/world3d/hero/materials.ts`, `src/world3d/hero/faceTexture.ts`, `src/world3d/hero/hero.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Appearance`, `APPEARANCE_OPTIONS`, `sanitizeAppearance`, and `CATALOGUE` from `src/features/shop/catalogue.ts`.
- Produces:
  - `type PartSpec = { slot: AppearancePart | 'head' | 'hands' | 'arms' | 'legs' | 'face'; bone: BoneName; shape: string; colour: string; offset: [number,number,number]; scale: [number,number,number]; rotation?: [number,number,number] }`.
  - `type BoneName = 'chest' | 'spine' | 'head' | 'upperarm.l' | 'upperarm.r' | 'lowerarm.l' | 'lowerarm.r' | 'hand.l' | 'hand.r' | 'handslot.r' | 'handslot.l' | 'upperleg.l' | 'upperleg.r' | 'lowerleg.l' | 'lowerleg.r' | 'foot.l' | 'foot.r'`.

  - `faceTexture(eyes, face): CanvasTexture | null`, which returns null when `document.createElement('canvas').getContext('2d')` is null (jsdom).

**Rules the implementer cannot infer:**
- **Proportions**, measured from the Knight rig rest pose (units = rig units; the scene scales the hero by 0.5):
  - head bone at y 1.241: head is a rounded box about 1.0 × 0.95 × 0.95, centred 0.5 above the bone;
  - chest at 0.973: torso about 0.8 × 0.6 × 0.6;
  - arms run along +x in the rest pose from `upperarm.*` (±0.212) to `hand.*` (±0.787);
  - legs run from `upperleg.*` (y 0.519) to `foot.*` (y 0.145);
  - the model faces +z.
- **Orient each limb part** along its child bone's local `position` vector. Don't assume an axis.
- **Shapes** are primitives (RoundedBox from `three/examples/jsm/geometries/RoundedBoxGeometry.js`, capsules, cones, spheres, tori). Each slot value gets a visibly different silhouette or colour:
  - **hairStyle:** one mesh group per style (the hijab also covers the neck);
  - **top:** a colour plus a shape family:
    - tunic, vest, hoodie and scarf are the torso box plus a detail (vest panel, hood, scarf torus);
    - robe and dress add a flared cone skirt from the hips;
  - **bottom, shoes:** colour plus shape;
  - **accessory:** mounted on the head (glasses on the face front);
  - **weapon:** at `handslot.r`;
  - **backpack:** on the back of the chest (−z);
  - **body a, b, c:** torso width 1.0, 1.1 and 0.92;
  - **all 11 shop items** follow the shapes in `renderCharacterSvg.ts` (for example `owl_pack` is an owl head over the left shoulder, `winged_boots` have outward wings, `star_cloak` is a back cape with star decals).

### Task 7: The 3D world scene

**Files:**
- Create in `src/world3d/`:
  - `World3D.tsx` (the Canvas, lazily exported default)
  - `Ground.tsx` (instanced tiles per `TileKind`, cliff skirt)
  - `Water.tsx` (vertex-wave and foam shader)
  - `Props.tsx` (one `InstancedMesh` per prop GLB sub-mesh, wind sway via `onBeforeCompile` on tree materials)
  - `Actors.tsx` (hero, NPCs, monsters, boss)
  - `CameraRig.tsx`
  - `TapRing.tsx`
  - `input.ts` (keyboard reader)
  - `world3d.test.tsx`
- Modify: `src/features/world/GameCanvas.tsx` (lazy `World3D` with WebGL check and error boundary), `src/features/world/WorldPage.tsx` (quality toggle button beside Home), and all 7 locale files.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `WorldSim` (Task 6), `HeroModel` (Task 4), `buildMonster` / `monsterMotion` (Task 5), `projection` and `quality` (Task 2), `useGame`, `worldPaused`.
- Produces:
  - DEV only, `window.__world = { screenOf(p: Point): { x: number; y: number } }`: projects a grid point to CSS pixels on the canvas.
  - i18n keys:
    - `world.noWebgl`: "Your device can't show the 3D world. Try another browser or device."
    - `world.loadFailed`: "The world didn't load. Check your connection."
    - `world.retry`: "Try again"
    - `world.quality`: "Graphics: {{level}}"
    - `world.qualityLevel.auto|high|low`: "Auto" / "High" / "Low"
- Behaviour:
  - Every frame, `useFrame` calls `sim.update(...)` and then writes object transforms through refs. The store updates `player` and `monsters` every 150 ms (as before), and `prompt` when it changes.
  - Hero scale is 0.5. Facing eases toward the movement angle, unless reduced motion is on.
  - Tap: raycast to the y = 0 plane → `worldToGrid` → `sim.tap`, and show the TapRing there.
  - Camera: follow lerp 0.12 per frame at 60 fps, scaled by dt (instant under reduced motion). Mouse wheel and two-finger pinch change the distance within `CAMERA.minDistance..maxDistance`.
  - Interact: keys E, Space and Enter, plus `interactSeq`. Same `panelClosedAt` 300 ms guard as the old scene.
  - `frameloop` is `'never'` while `battle || boss` is set, `'always'` otherwise.
  - NPCs: `HeroModel` with their `NPCS` appearance. They play `wave` once when the hero first enters `INTERACT_RANGE`.
  - Monsters: `monsterMotion('idle'|'hop')` while wandering, `'poof'` over 0.5 s on defeat, and a pop-in scale from 0 to 1 over 0.3 s on respawn.
  - Hero one-shots: `cheer` when `cheerSeq` changes, `hit` when `hitSeq` changes. Both play after the panel closes, and only the last one queued plays.
  - Quality:
    - `'auto'` runs `createQualityMonitor`; on `'low'`, set `dpr` to 1.5, turn shadows off and drop the sparkles.
    - The toggle cycles auto → high → low and saves with `saveQualityChoice`.
  - Lighting: a hemisphere fill (sky `#bfe6ff`, ground `#8bc34a`) and one warm directional sun (`#fff1d6`) casting shadows on a 2048 map (1024 on low). The background is `#5fb3d9`, as now.
  - Things no pack provides are built in code in the same style:
    - `bush`: a cluster of 3–4 squashed spheres;
    - `bridge` tiles: plank boxes with rope posts;
    - `flowers` tiles: small instanced petals;
